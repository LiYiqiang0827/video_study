## Canonical frames and metadata

Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`, not pretend timestamps. Timestamps are ISO with `T` and explicit `+08:00`; timezone metadata is `Asia/Shanghai`. No naive timestamps or UTC strings in canonical frames. Prices are positive finite CNY/share; vol/amount are finite nonnegative, including legitimate zero volume. OHLC must contain open/close in low..high. Nulls, duplicate keys, descending keys, extra columns and overlapping intervals are errors. Empty frames retain exact columns; no bars are manufactured.

`RawBundle` fields: `ts_code`, `daily`, `factors`, `metadata`, optional `one_min`, `fifteen_min`, `calendar`, `labels={}`. RawBundle is privileged engineering data: it can contain full cached history and is NEVER handed to a renderer or restoration analyst. `load_raw` receives cutoff from v0.1 and is responsible for applying the request to source queries where practical; a full raw cache may be read internally. `build_daily_view`/E must slice before any calculations, labels, annotations, serialization or rendering; they are the mandatory read-layer safety boundary even if a synthetic or cache RawBundle contains future rows.

Factor dates without daily bars MUST survive raw caching. Exact daily factor matches required; absent factors raise `MISSING_FACTOR`, never implicit 1 or forward fill. Existing minute `name` and `adj_factor` columns do not establish historical label/factor availability. Adapters discard those fields from canonical bars and separately verify lineage.

`RawMetadata` and `SeriesMetadata` exact required keys are exported in `contracts.py`. Series keys are daily/factors/one_min/fifteen_min. Unit metadata records original units, conversion scale and evidence references. Daily source vol in lots multiplies by 100; daily amount in thousand CNY multiplies by 1000. TDX OHLC minutes use shares and CNY, both scale 1. Unknown CSV units block the affected series (`UNIT_UNVERIFIED`), not a guess from column names. Price/factor source family must match for daily. The factors SeriesMetadata retains canonical normalized unit fields with neutral original units/scales; only its factor values are consumed.

Raw `source_version` is an opaque revision/content digest of all cache components (not merely max date). Series source_version identifies each actual snapshot. Raw cache metadata may describe full coverage; view metadata must not. Calendar source/version and coverage must identify its verified exchange (SSE evidence may cover shared mainland sessions only after root acceptance). Calendar unavailable outside its declared range is not silently extrapolated. Root sample calendar presently covers 2018-01-01..2026-09-30. Calendar gaps do not remove known 2017 daily bars from independent learning.

## Daily cutoff v0.1 and fixed anchor

At requested cutoff before 15:00, retain only actual daily dates STRICTLY BEFORE the cutoff civil date. At or after 15:00, permit actual daily dates <= cutoff civil date. This safe bound is not an exchange-calendar assertion: do not manufacture prior exchange days or assume weekdays are open. `effective_cutoff` is the safe civil-date bound at 15:00 (or the latest confirmed completed exchange date when calendar evidence is present), independently of the last observed stock bar. This permits a factor-only anchor during a stock-specific data gap; RenderManifest actual_end separately reports real coverage. Warn `INTRADAY_DAY_OMITTED` for cutoff before 15:00 and `CALENDAR_UNAVAILABLE` when gap/calendar interpretation is unavailable. Before opening and during lunch, v0.1 still omits the entire current day. A full calendar can confirm market dates without changing this conservative rule.

Choose the latest positive factor date <= the safe completed-date bound, including factor-only dates. Only the factors needed for visible prices and eligible anchor enter the view's factor digest. Compute each visible OHLC as `raw_price * row_factor / anchor_factor`. `vol` and `amount` remain exactly their normalized raw values. Window selection must not discard factors needed for the selected rows or anchor. A later factor 8 cannot change a view anchored at an earlier factor 4. Missing eligible anchor raises `MISSING_FACTOR` for nonempty input. Empty views may have factor_lineage=null.

For intraday E, date-only factors anchor no later than the previous completed date. A current-day factor may be used only with independent available_at evidence <= effective cutoff. A changed current-day factor without independent availability evidence blocks current-day adjusted bars; do not blindly reuse a previous factor after a split. A verified unchanged current-day factor can be established by prior factor lineage/evidence; E must record the proof. No later factors enter visible prices, indicators, labels, warnings or content digests. Opaque source snapshot version is permitted; future coverage/max dates/counts are forbidden in a sanitized view.

`ChartView` exact fields: ts_code, period, bars, metadata. `ViewMetadata` exports exact keys, including stage, window, requested/effective cutoff, cutoff source/policy, units, source_version/source_ids, factor_lineage, labels/labels_missing, incomplete, warnings and gap_dates. Daily bars normally use DAILY_COLUMNS; E daily bars use MINUTE_COLUMNS for every row, with historical interval 09:30..15:00 and partial current-day interval 09:30..last included minute end. ViewMetadata must have no undeclared fields. Historical label values require effective_date and available_at <= effective cutoff; unknown name/ST/themes/boards are absent, with the key in labels_missing. Current labels are never substituted. Warnings can describe visible input limitations only. `ChartView.to_dict()` serializes the exact four fields, with bars=`{columns:[...],records:[...]}`; `ChartView.from_dict()` reconstructs and validates this exact JSON format without sorting or filling.

## Minute intervals, source compatibility and partial bars

Calendar coverage is mandatory for E. On a confirmed exchange-open date: preopen cutoff resolves to previous confirmed open date 15:00; lunch [11:30,13:00) resolves to 11:30; postclose resolves to 15:00; during session use the actual cutoff. Closed civil dates resolve to previous confirmed open date 15:00. Calendar must include that prior date. Source endpoints 09:31..11:30 and 13:01..15:00 describe intervals `(bar_start,bar_end]`; first interval is 09:30..09:31. The minute evidence has no within-minute seconds: include ONLY `bar_end <= effective_cutoff`. At 10:07:30 include 10:07, exclude 10:08; do not prorate a complete minute.

15m bins start at 09:30,09:45,...,11:15 and 13:00,...,14:45. Complete bins end after 15 minutes. A partial bin is aggregated from included 1m bars only and ends at their last bar_end, e.g. 10:00..10:07 at 10:07:30. It has is_partial=true. Daily partial uses the same included minutes and is_partial=true before close. Complete OHLC uses first open, max high, min low, last close; vol and amount sum. Do not label a gap as suspension or pretend an incomplete source period is complete. Gaps require visible warnings/evidence; no data filling. The intraday view consumes the same canonical one-minute rows as 1m; renderer can display close line plus corresponding minute volume.

TDX native 15m first bar includes opening auction; one-minute CSV conversion removes 09:30. This difference MUST be disclosed and evaluated in overlap evidence. Do not combine full native 15m bars with partial 1m bars whose auction policy/source compatibility is unverified. Unknown stock-day lineage in mixed TDX/CSV partitions raises `SOURCE_UNVERIFIED`.

`RawMetadata.compatibility` per minute series uses exact `SourceCompatibility` fields. All unit/price-basis/factor-provenance flags must be true, evidence refs present, source IDs must match actual series and auction_policy must be same or verified_difference. same_source requires equal actual source families. TDX and Tushare are different families: use verified_overlap only after objective checks of matched dates, normalized volume/amount, unadjusted OHLC basis and attached factor provenance. checked_trade_dates and evidence refs are mandatory. Equality of minute aggregation with native 15m is internal consistency, not proof of original source. E fails closed until root accepts this evidence; independent daily PNG may proceed.

All periods share the identical requested/effective cutoff and factor anchor where included in a combined minute view. Historical daily data remains closed; current daily is synthesized only from included one-minute evidence. Never use the eventual current daily close or full native crossing 15m bar. E must check all metadata before returning any combined output.

## Rendering and caching

RenderOptions defaults: 3840x1600 px, 160 dpi, Microsoft YaHei, rising #d62728, falling #2ca02c, ma_periods=(). C does no reading, price adjustment, unit normalization or label lookup. It validates, does not mutate bars/metadata, retains every candle/volume bar and returns RenderManifest. Empty input returns status=empty, output_path=null, zero counts, null actual_start/end, no PNG. Errors raise ChartError. A doji can use rising color. Enabled MAs are simple arithmetic means of visible closes with min_periods=N, no future bars and no invented prior history; report visible-prefix insufficiency.

RenderManifest carries ts_code, period, status, output_path, input_rows, candle_count, volume_bar_count, actual_start/end, view_identity, timings, warnings and contract_version. timings exact keys are drawing_seconds and encoding_seconds, measured with perf_counter. Drawing includes artist preparation and canvas draw; encoding includes PNG compression/write from already-drawn canvas. Empty status reports both0. Root separately measures full render/CLI elapsed time; do not label draw-only time as complete PNG time. actual coverage uses daily dates or minute bar_end. view_identity includes all ViewMetadata and bars_sha256, therefore carries period, cutoff, source/confidence, units, window, stage, source version, anchor/lineage, historical labels, incomplete state and warnings. A processed view cache key is SHA256 of canonical JSON of this identity; raw cache is separate per stock/components/source version. Never reuse a restore view as reveal/study, or one anchor/cutoff as another. Raw and view caches use temporary files, validation, then atomic replace under one writer per stock partition. Historical source revisions invalidate affected stock cache even when max date is unchanged.

Numbers in raw-cache round trips must be exact where original encoding permits. Price transformation/independent validation tolerances: rtol=1e-12, atol=1e-10 CNY/share. Volume and amount must equal the normalized input exactly (atol=0); aggregated totals use the same specified summation order. No tolerance increase merely to pass. Volume must not be factor-adjusted.
